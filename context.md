ShyText—it’s like a secret social radar for meeting people nearby without the awkwardness. Imagine this:

You’re at a coffee shop, a concert, or even on the metro, and you see someone you’d love to talk to… but you’re too shy to walk up to them. With ShyText, you just open the app, and it shows you profiles of people around you (5 meter radius) with their photos and a little bio. If you like someone’s vibe, you can send them a quick message—no stalking, no guessing, no pressure.

Here’s the cool part:

It’s all real-time: You see who’s physically near you right now, like a heatmap of humans.

No creepy tracking: People only show up if they’re actively using the app or recently checked in (like a “last seen 5 mins ago” tag).

Super private: You control if you’re “visible” or in Ghost Mode (invisible but still able to message others).

Think of it like leaving a digital sticky note for someone nearby. For example:

Spot someone reading your favorite book? Send them a quote from it.

See someone at a party with the same band T-shirt? Slide into their DMs with a “🤘”.

Want to meet people at a conference? ShyText becomes your icebreaker.

And the best part? No swiping, no algorithms—just real people, right next to you. It’s like Tinder meets Pokémon Go, but for making IRL connections.
