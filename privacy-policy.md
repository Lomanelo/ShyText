# Privacy Policy for ShyText

Last Updated: July 14, 2023

## Introduction

Welcome to ShyText. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you about how we look after your personal data when you use our mobile application (ShyText) and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

## Who We Are

**Developer:** ShyText (Independent Developer)
**Email:** shytext.info@gmail.com

## The Data We Collect About You

We may collect, use, store, and transfer different kinds of personal data about you as follows:

### Information You Provide to Us:

- **Identity Data**: First name, username or similar identifier, profile picture
- **Contact Data**: Email address (when using Google sign-in)
- **Profile Data**: Your profile information, including bio, interests, and other information you choose to share
- **User Content**: Messages you send to other users

### Information We Automatically Collect:

- **Location Data**: Your precise geographic location (only when you use the app and have granted permission)
- **Device Data**: Information about your mobile device, including device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data**: Information about how you use our application, including time spent on the app and features used
- **Technical Data**: Internet protocol (IP) address, device type, mobile network information

### Special Categories of Personal Data:

We do not knowingly collect any Special Categories of Personal Data about you (this includes details about your race or ethnicity, religious or philosophical beliefs, sex life, sexual orientation, political opinions, trade union membership, information about your health, and genetic and biometric data).

## How We Use Your Data

We use your information for the following purposes:

- To create and manage your account
- To provide our core functionality of proximity-based messaging
- To connect you with other users based on location
- To display your profile to other users within your area
- To deliver and improve our services
- To communicate with you about updates or changes to our service
- To protect the security and integrity of our platform
- To comply with legal obligations

## Data Sharing and Disclosures

We may share your personal information with:

- **Other Users**: Your profile information and location data will be shared with other users nearby to facilitate connections. Your messages will be shared with the intended recipients.

- **Service Providers**: We use third-party service providers to help operate this application (such as hosting, analytics, and authentication services).

- **Legal Requirements**: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

- **Business Transfers**: If the ShyText app is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction.

## Data Storage and Security

We store your data on Firebase services, which include the Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Authentication. Your data is primarily stored in the United States. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized or unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, or damage.

## Third-Party Services

Our application uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

- **Firebase** (Google): For user authentication, database, and file storage
- **Google Sign-In**: For authentication purposes
- **Expo**: For building and deployment of the application

Please note that these third-party services have their own privacy policies that govern their use of your information.

## Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location, you may have various rights regarding your personal data, including:

- **Access**: The right to request copies of your personal data
- **Rectification**: The right to request correction of any inaccurate information
- **Erasure**: The right to request deletion of your personal data
- **Restriction**: The right to request restriction of processing of your personal data
- **Portability**: The right to request transfer of your personal data
- **Objection**: The right to object to processing of your personal data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the contact details provided.

## Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will promptly delete that information.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

## Location Data Specific Provisions

ShyText requires access to your precise location data to provide its core functionality of connecting you with nearby users. Your location data will be:

- Collected only when the app is in use (or in the background with appropriate permissions)
- Uploaded to our servers to calculate proximity to other users
- Used to show your approximate location to other users
- Retained only as long as necessary for the functioning of the application

You can disable location sharing at any time through your device settings, but this will limit functionality of the application.

## Your Legal Rights in Different Jurisdictions

### For European Users (GDPR)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights governed by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Our legal basis for collecting and using your personal information depends on the specific information concerned and the context in which we collect it.

### For California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You have the right to request disclosure of what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell. You also have the right to request deletion of your personal information collected by us, and the right to opt-out of the sale of your personal information.

### For Users in Other Jurisdictions

We are committed to respecting your rights under applicable data protection laws in your jurisdiction. Please contact us if you have specific questions related to data protection laws in your location.
