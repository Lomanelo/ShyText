Canonical text: `src/lib/legal.ts` and `public/privacy.html` (keep those in sync).

**Developer:** independent developer  
**Email:** hello@shytext.com

We collect account data, venue presence (not shared as precise GPS), notes, messages, reports, and push tokens. Chat notifications may show a short message preview; ShyText request banners do not include the note. On iOS, a Live Activity may show the venue name on the Lock Screen while you are Shyned. Account deletion in the app removes associated personal profile data; existing chats may remain with the other participant.
